Common Questions

Good to know before you start.

What happens during a demo?

We'll walk you through the platform based on your business and answer any questions you have, no obligation to sign up.

What happens after the demo?

If you'd like to go ahead, the team will jump on an onboarding call with you to help get everything set up.

Are the prices in NZD?

Yes. All pricing is in New Zealand dollars and excludes GST.

What counts as a user?

A user is anyone with a login to the OSHE platform. Workers who sign in via QR code on site do not count as users.

Is HARM consulting included in my OSHE subscription?

Launch and Growth include customised OHSM policies and procedures, onboarding, quarterly check-ins, ongoing support and advice, and incident investigation remote support. Extra consulting such as site reviews and Totika advisory work is available at member rates.

How does Enterprise pricing work?

Enterprise is priced by negotiation based on your organisation's size and needs. Get in touch with Vanessa to talk through what would work for you.
