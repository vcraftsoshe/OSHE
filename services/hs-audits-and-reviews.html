Common questions

What do you actually hand over?

A plain-language action plan you can act on, not only a report that sits in a drawer.

Is the review independent?

Yes. The services page calls it an independent look at where your H&S stands.

How is an audit priced?

Per engagement, based on organisation size and scope. The pricing page says to get in touch for a quote. No figure is published here.

Do OSHE members pay the same?

The pricing page says members get preferential rates on extra consulting. The rate itself is not listed on the site.

Is this a certification?

No. The site describes an audit and a clear action plan. It does not describe a certification scheme.
