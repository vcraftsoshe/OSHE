Mobile App

Works offline. Built for site, not the office.

Book a free demo Back to OSHE features

The OSHE mobile app keeps working without internet. Fill in forms, including incident notifications, complete task analyses with their hazards, and add tasks. Everything syncs when you get back in range. The desktop handles back-end management and reporting. The mobile app handles the work on site, and you install it on your phone from the browser.

  • Forms, task analyses and tasks work offline
  • Auto-sync when back online
  • Incident notification form available offline
  • Hazards logged inside task analyses, even with no signal
  • Desktop handles back-end management and reporting
  • Install on any smartphone from the browser

Common questions

Does the mobile app need a signal?

Not for the core site work. Forms (including incident notifications), task analyses, tasks and quick jobs are saved on the phone with no signal. Downloading the SSSP, the visitor logs and the file browser need an internet connection.

When does the data reach the office?

It syncs when the phone is back online.

What is the desktop for?

Back-end management and reporting, including the registers. The mobile app is for the work on site.

Do we install it from an app store?

No. It is a web app you install on your phone from the browser, then log in with your OSHE account.

Is site sign-in the same as the mobile app?

No. Site sign-in is the QR flow and does not need an app download. The mobile app is for forms, task analyses and tasks.
