Smart Notifications

Set it. Forget it. Let OSHE do the nudging.

Book a free demo Back to OSHE features

Keeping track of what is due is one of the biggest admin headaches in H&S. OSHE does the nudging for you. Admins get a task before someone's training expires. Incidents email the right people straight away. Tasks email the person they are assigned to, and repeating tasks set themselves up again. Insurance, accreditation and data sheet expiry dates all sit on the registers, so you can see what is coming.

  • Training and induction expiry reminders as admin tasks
  • Instant incident emails to the admins and supervisors you choose
  • Email when a task is assigned to you
  • Repeating tasks on a schedule or when the last one closes
  • Email when a contractor submits their prequalification
  • Insurance, accreditation and data sheet expiry dates on the registers

Common questions

What does OSHE remind us about?

Training expiry. Each day OSHE checks training records and gives admins who opt in a task when someone's training is close to expiring. OSHE also emails people when a task is assigned to them, when an incident is reported, and when a contractor submits their prequalification.

Who receives the reminder?

Training expiry tasks go to the admins who have opted in. Incident emails go to the admins and supervisors who have opted in. Task emails go to the person the task is assigned to.

Does OSHE remind us when contractor insurance or a data sheet expires?

Not automatically. OSHE records insurance, accreditation and safety data sheet expiry dates on the registers and in the contractor export, so you can see what is coming up. You can also set a repeating task to check them.

Can OSHE set up regular H&amp;S tasks for us?

Yes. Repeating tasks can run on a schedule, or start again when the last one is closed.

Is this a separate calendar we have to maintain?

No. Reminders and tasks come from the records already in OSHE.
