Incident Reporting

Report at sign-out. Notify the right people.

Book a free demo Back to OSHE features

When workers sign out for the day, they are prompted to flag any incidents. The right people get notified automatically. Corrective actions can be assigned and tracked through to closure. Privacy controls mean only the people who need to see a report can see it. On average, teams see an 80% increase in incidents reported.

  • Incident flagging built into the sign-out flow
  • Automatic notifications to the right people
  • Corrective actions assigned and tracked to closure
  • Privacy controlled throughout
  • Reportable from the desktop too

Common questions

When do workers report an incident?

They are prompted to flag incidents when they sign out for the day. Reports can also be made from the desktop.

Who gets told?

The right people are notified automatically.

What happens after a report is in?

Corrective actions can be assigned and tracked through to closure.

Can everyone on the account read every report?

No. Privacy controls mean only the people who need to see it can see it.

Has reporting changed for customers?

On average, teams see an 80% increase in incidents reported.
