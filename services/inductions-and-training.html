Inductions and Training

Training library with available modules. Add your own on top.

Book a free demo Back to OSHE features

Most organisations hand someone a form on day one and call it an induction. OSHE has a training library with available modules, so people understand what incident reporting, hazard management, and your H&S policy mean in practice. Add your own modules on top. Two-step sign-off keeps it accountable.

  • Training library with available modules
  • Add your own custom induction modules
  • Site inductions done in the QR sign-in flow
  • Two-step sign-off by worker and manager
  • Training register with expiry reminders sent to admins as tasks
  • Certificates stored and accessible by each user

Common questions

Do we have to write every induction from scratch?

No. The training library has available modules you can add to your own register. You can add your own modules on top.

Where do people complete an induction?

Site inductions happen in the QR sign-in flow on their first visit to site. Training and induction modules are recorded against each person, with their sign-off.

How is sign-off handled?

Two-step sign-off by the worker and the manager.

What about certificates that expire?

Each person's training record stores the certificate and its expiry date. Admins who opt in get a task before someone's training expires.

What do the available modules cover?

They are there so people understand incident reporting, hazard management, and your H&S policy in practice.
