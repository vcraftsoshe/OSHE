QR Sign In and Out

No app needed for site sign-in. Just a phone camera.

Book a free demo Back to OSHE features

Post a QR code at the site entrance. Workers and visitors scan it with their phone camera, sign in, and first timers complete the site induction in the same flow. No app download, no account creation, and no friction for people coming on site. Admins and supervisors can see exactly who is there.

  • Works with any smartphone camera, no app download needed for sign-in
  • Site induction completed in the same scan-in flow
  • Sign in and out tracked in real time
  • Visitor log for admins and supervisors, with download
  • Automatic sign-out after 12 hours if someone forgets
  • Sign-out lets workers flag any incidents

Common questions

Do people need to download an app to sign in?

No. They scan the site QR code with their phone camera and sign in on a web page. There is no app download and no account needed.

When is the induction done?

On their first visit. First timers complete the site induction form in the same scan-in flow. Returning visitors just sign in.

Can we see who is on site?

Yes. Sign in and out are tracked as they happen. Admins and supervisors can view and download the visitor log for each job.

What if someone forgets to sign out?

OSHE signs them out automatically after 12 hours. Admins can also sign people out.

What happens at sign-out?

Workers can report an incident as they sign out. The admins and supervisors who have opted in get an email straight away.
