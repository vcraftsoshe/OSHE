Contractor Management

Book a free demo Back to OSHE features

Getting a new contractor on site used to mean chasing paperwork for weeks. Add a contractor and OSHE emails them a link to your prequalification form. Their answers and documents come back into the system, into their own folder, and the person who manages that contractor is told it is ready to review. Every insurance and accreditation expiry date sits on the register, so you can see what is coming up.

  • Prequalification link emailed as soon as you add a contractor
  • All documents stored and organised per contractor
  • Insurance and accreditation expiry dates on the register and in the export
  • Owner assigned per contractor for accountability
  • Review dates and update requests per contractor
  • Full contractor register built automatically

Common questions

How does a new contractor get into OSHE?

Add the contractor with their company name and email. OSHE emails them a secure link to your prequalification form. They fill it in without an OSHE login, and the person managing that contractor gets an email when it is submitted.

Where are contractor documents kept?

Each contractor gets their own folder in your OSHE files. Their answers and documents also sit on the contractor register.

What happens when insurance or a certificate is due?

OSHE records the expiry date for each insurance and accreditation. You can see them on the contractor register and in the CSV export. OSHE does not send an automatic reminder for these dates.

Who is responsible for each contractor?

Each contractor has a named person managing them, so accountability sits with a person, not a shared inbox.

How do we review a contractor again later?

Set a review date for each contractor. When something needs updating, send an update request from OSHE and the contractor gets an email with your reason.
