Hazard and Safety Registers

All your registers, linked and kept up to date.

Book a free demo Back to OSHE features

OSHE holds linked registers for hazards, PPE, plant and equipment, and hazardous substances, plus emergency procedures and training. Every hazard change is logged with who made it and when, so you have an audit trail. Safety data sheets sit on the hazardous substances register with their expiry dates, so you can pull one up from any device logged in to OSHE instead of digging through a folder in the ute. Hazard, plant, substance and training registers download as print-ready PDFs.

  • Hazard, PPE, plant and equipment, and hazardous substances
  • Pre-loaded hazard library to get started fast
  • Change history on every hazard
  • Safety data sheets and expiry dates on the substances register
  • Comments and review ticks against hazard records
  • Print-ready PDF downloads

Common questions

Which registers are in OSHE?

Hazards, PPE, plant and equipment, hazardous substances, emergency procedures and training. You choose which ones apply to each job.

Can we see who changed a record?

Yes for hazards. Every hazard change is logged with who made it and when, along with any comment and a hazard reviewed tick.

How do we start a hazard register?

Copy hazards from the pre-loaded library into your own register, then add your own. Each hazard has risks, an initial risk rating, controls and a residual risk rating.

Where are hazardous substance data sheets?

On the hazardous substances register, with the expiry date for each sheet. Open it from any device logged in to OSHE.

Can we print a register?

Yes. The hazard, plant and equipment, hazardous substances and training registers download as print-ready PDFs.
