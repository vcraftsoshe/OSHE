OSHE Software · Contractors

Contractor management software for NZ businesses

No more chasing insurance certificates by email. Send one link, get everything back in one place, and know who is cleared to work.

Book a free demo See all OSHE features

How it works

  1. Add the contractor in OSHE with their company name and email.
  2. OSHE emails them a secure link to their prequalification form. They don't need an OSHE login.
  3. They fill it in and upload their documents. Everything lands in a folder for that contractor in your OSHE files.
  4. The person managing that contractor gets an email when it is submitted.
  5. Review it. Approve it, or send it back with a note about what needs updating. OSHE emails the contractor for you.
  6. Set a review date so you know when to check them again.

What the prequalification form collects

The business

Company name, key contact, scope of works and number of workers.

Accreditation

Whether they hold an existing accreditation, the type, the certificate and the expiry date.

Insurance

Public liability, professional indemnity, motor vehicle and other cover, each with its certificate and expiry date.

Their H&S system

H&S manual and policy, hazard register, risk procedure, incident process, training register, an example risk assessment, plant maintenance records, and notifiable work and contractor procedures if they apply. Plus a signed authorisation.

One register, always current

  • Every contractor in one register, with their status: pending, ready for review, approved, waiting on an update, or archived.
  • A named person manages each contractor, so it is clear who owns it.
  • Search and filter the list. Export it to CSV with every insurance and accreditation expiry date.
  • Share a contractor's form link by QR code as well as email.
  • A duplicate check when you add a contractor, so you don't end up with the same company twice.
  • Add contractors to a job's SSSP and tick when their own SSSP comes in.

On site, contractors sign in by scanning your QR code with their phone camera. No app download.

Contractor management questions, answered

What is contractor management in health and safety?

Contractor management is how a business checks, onboards and monitors the contractors it engages, so their work is done safely. It usually covers prequalification, insurance and accreditation checks, inductions, sign in on site, and reviewing contractors over time.

Do I have to check my contractors under HSWA?

Under the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015, businesses (PCBUs) that share duties must consult, co-operate and co-ordinate with each other so far as is reasonably practicable (section 34). WorkSafe says a PCBU cannot contract out of its duties. Checking a contractor's health and safety before they start is a common way to do your part.

What is contractor prequalification in NZ?

Prequalification is checking a contractor's health and safety systems before they work for you. In NZ that can be your own form or a recognised scheme such as Totika or SiteWise. Read more in our contractor prequalification guide.

What does OSHE collect from a contractor?

Company details, scope of works, number of workers, accreditation details, insurance certificates with expiry dates, and key H&S documents such as their policy, hazard register, incident process, training register and an example risk assessment.

Does the contractor need an OSHE login?

No. OSHE emails them a secure link to their own form. They fill it in and upload documents without creating an account.

How do I know when a contractor needs reviewing?

Set a review date for each contractor. It shows on the contractor register. When something needs fixing, send an update request from OSHE and the contractor gets an email with your reason.

Can HARM help with our own prequalification?

Yes. HARM helps businesses with prequalification applications for Totika, SiteWise, SHE and more. See prequalification support.
