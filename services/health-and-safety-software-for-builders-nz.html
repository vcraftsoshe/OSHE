OSHE Software · Builders

Health and safety software for NZ builders

Built for site, not the office. One place for your SSSPs, sign ins, toolbox talks, task analyses, subbies and incidents.

Book a free demo See all OSHE features

Before the job starts

  • Subbies prequalified. Send each contractor a link. Get their insurances, accreditations and H&S documents back in one place. How contractor management works.
  • SSSP done properly. Start from the OSHE SSSP template and pull in your registers, task analyses and contractors. How SSSPs work.
  • Registers ready. Hazards, PPE, plant and equipment (including test and tag and inspection dates), hazardous substances with data sheets, and emergency procedures.

On site

  • QR sign in. Put the job QR code at the gate. People scan it with their phone camera. First timers do the site induction, regulars just sign in. Anyone who forgets to sign out is signed out after 12 hours.
  • Toolbox talks and site reviews. Built in forms you fill in on your phone, saved against the job.
  • Task analyses. Break a task into steps, add hazards, risk ratings, controls, PPE, equipment and training. Reuse templates for the jobs you do every week.
  • No signal, no problem. The OSHE mobile app saves forms, task analyses and tasks on the phone and syncs when you are back in range.

When something goes wrong

  • Workers can report an incident when they sign out, or fill in the incident notification form.
  • Admins and supervisors who have opted in get an email straight away.
  • Corrective actions become tasks with an owner and a due date, tracked until they are done.

Staying on top of it

  • Training register for every worker, with tickets, expiry dates and competency. Admins who opt in get a task before someone's training expires.
  • Repeating tasks for the things that have to happen every week or month, like site reviews.
  • PDFs with your logo for forms, task analyses and most registers, ready for clients and auditors.

Builder H&S questions, answered

What health and safety software do NZ builders need?

Most NZ builders need a way to produce SSSPs, sign people in and out of site, induct workers and visitors, run toolbox talks and site reviews, complete task analyses, report incidents, and check subcontractors. OSHE does all of these in one system.

Does OSHE work offline on site?

Yes. The OSHE mobile app saves forms, task analyses, tasks and quick jobs on the phone when there is no signal, and syncs them when you are back online.

Do subcontractors need to download an app to sign in?

No. Subcontractors and visitors scan the site QR code with their phone camera and sign in on a web page. No app and no account needed.

Can I run toolbox talks in OSHE?

Yes. OSHE has a built in toolbox talk form. Talks are saved against the job and recent ones are included in the job's SSSP.

What is a task analysis?

A task analysis (often called a JSA) breaks a job into steps, lists the hazards in each step, rates the risk, and sets the controls. In OSHE a task analysis also records the PPE, equipment and training needed, and can be saved as a template.

What do builders have to report to WorkSafe?

Under HSWA, notifiable events must be reported to WorkSafe as soon as possible after you become aware of them. Notifiable events are deaths, notifiable injuries or illnesses, and notifiable incidents. If you are not sure whether something is notifiable, HARM can help.

Is OSHE only for big building companies?

No. The Launch plan covers up to 10 users and Growth up to 20. Larger companies can talk to us about Enterprise. See pricing.

Who is behind OSHE?

OSHE is health and safety software made in New Zealand, based in Papamoa. It sits alongside HARM, our consulting team, for when you want an expert as well as the software.
