Common questions

What is an SSSP in OSHE?

It is the site safety plan for the job. Every job in OSHE starts from the same SSSP template instead of a blank document, and OSHE builds it into one PDF with your logo.

What gets pulled into the plan?

The task analyses and templates for the job, the registers and people you pick (plant and equipment, emergency procedures, hazardous substances, training), the contractor list, and recent toolbox talks, site reviews and incident notifications for the job.

Can the plan be updated during the job?

Yes. Regenerate it whenever things change. Earlier versions stay on the job.

Can people open it on site?

Yes. The latest SSSP can be downloaded from the OSHE mobile app when the phone has an internet connection.

Why do teams use this instead of a fresh document each time?

Building an SSSP from scratch usually takes a couple of hours. OSHE starts from your own task analyses, registers and the SSSP template.
