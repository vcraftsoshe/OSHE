Questions

Health and safety software NZ: common questions

What is OSHE?

OSHE is health and safety software made in New Zealand. It covers contractor management, QR site sign in, site safety plans (SSSPs), inductions and training, incident reporting, hazard and safety registers, task analyses, and a mobile app that works offline. See SSSP templates, contractor management and H&S software for builders.

What is HARM?

HARM is the consulting side of OSHE. It offers prequalification support, H&S audits and reviews, policy and procedure writing, general H&S support, and 1:1 sessions with Vanessa Crafts. No retainer needed.

Who is OSHE for?

New Zealand businesses that want to run their own health and safety system. That includes builders, trades, and any business that engages contractors.

Do workers need to download an app to sign in to site?

No. Workers, contractors and visitors scan the site QR code with their phone camera and sign in on a web page. First timers complete the site induction in the same flow.

Does OSHE work without internet?

Yes. The OSHE mobile app saves forms, task analyses and tasks on the phone when there is no signal, and syncs them when you are back online.

Does OSHE make us compliant with HSWA?

OSHE gives you the tools to manage risks, keep records and work with your contractors, which are core parts of the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015. Software on its own does not make a business compliant. If you want an expert to check your setup, HARM can help.

How much does OSHE cost?

Launch is $179 per month plus GST for up to 10 users. Growth is $249 per month plus GST for up to 20 users. Enterprise is priced by negotiation. Every plan starts with a free, no obligation demo.
