01
Contractor Management

Getting a new contractor on site used to mean chasing paperwork for weeks. Add a contractor and OSHE emails them a link to your prequalification form. Their answers and documents come back into the system, into their own folder, and the person who manages that contractor is told it is ready to review. Every insurance and accreditation expiry date sits on the register, so you can see what is coming up.

  • Prequalification link emailed as soon as you add a contractor
  • All documents stored and organised per contractor
  • Insurance and accreditation expiry dates on the register and in the export
  • Owner assigned per contractor for accountability
  • Review dates and update requests per contractor
  • Full contractor register built automatically
02
QR Sign In and Out

No app needed for site sign-in. Just a phone camera.

Post a QR code at the site entrance. Workers and visitors scan it with their phone camera, sign in, and complete their induction in the same flow. No app download, no account creation, no friction for people coming on site. You get instant visibility of exactly who is there at any given moment.

  • Works with any smartphone camera, no app download needed for sign-in
  • Induction completed in the same scan-in flow
  • Sign in and out tracked in real time
  • Visitor log for admins and supervisors, with download
  • Automatic sign-out after 12 hours if someone forgets
  • Sign-out prompts workers to flag any incidents
03
Job Management and SSSP

Site Safety Plans in minutes, not hours.

Most SSSPs get copied from the last job. OSHE starts every job from the same SSSP template, links your task analyses and registers, and builds the document for you as one PDF with your logo. Regenerate it as the job moves and the latest toolbox talks, site reviews and incident notifications come with it. How SSSPs work in OSHE.

  • Same SSSP template for every job, so nothing gets missed
  • Task analysis templates linked to each job
  • Registers, people and contractors picked per job and pulled in
  • Toolbox talks, site reviews and incidents included
  • Every version kept on the job
  • Latest SSSP downloadable from the mobile app
04
Incident Reporting

Report at sign-out. Notify instantly.

When workers sign out for the day, they are prompted to flag any incidents. The right people get notified automatically. Corrective actions can be assigned and tracked right through to closure. Privacy controls mean only the people who need to see it can see it. On average, teams see an 80% increase in incidents reported.

  • Incident flagging built into the sign-out flow
  • Automatic notifications to the right people
  • Corrective actions assigned and tracked to closure
  • Privacy controlled throughout
  • Reportable from the desktop too
05
Inductions and Training

Available modules to start with. Fully customisable on top.

Most organisations hand someone a form on day one and call it an induction. OSHE has a training library with available modules so people actually understand what incident reporting, hazard management, and your H&S policy mean in practice. Add your own modules on top. Two-step sign-off keeps everything accountable.

  • Training library with available modules
  • Add your own custom induction modules
  • Site inductions done in the QR sign-in flow
  • Two-step sign-off by worker and manager
  • Training register with expiry reminders sent to admins as tasks
  • Certificates stored and accessible by each user
06
Hazard and Safety Registers

All your registers. Linked, tracked, and always up to date.

OSHE holds linked registers for hazards, PPE, plant and equipment, and hazardous substances, plus emergency procedures and training. Every hazard change is logged with who made it and when, so you have an audit trail. Safety data sheets sit on the hazardous substances register with their expiry dates, so you can pull one up from any device logged in to OSHE instead of digging through a folder in the ute. Hazard, plant, substance and training registers download as print-ready PDFs.

  • Hazard, PPE, plant and equipment, and hazardous substances
  • Pre-loaded hazard library to get started fast
  • Change history on every hazard
  • Safety data sheets and expiry dates on the substances register
  • Comments and review ticks against hazard records
  • Print-ready PDF downloads
07
Smart Notifications

Set it. Forget it. Stay on top without the mental load.

Keeping track of what is due is one of the biggest admin headaches in H&S. OSHE does the nudging for you. Admins get a task before someone's training expires. Incidents email the right people straight away. Tasks email the person they are assigned to, and repeating tasks set themselves up again. Insurance, accreditation and data sheet expiry dates all sit on the registers, so you can see what is coming.

  • Training and induction expiry reminders as admin tasks
  • Instant incident emails to the admins and supervisors you choose
  • Email when a task is assigned to you
  • Repeating tasks on a schedule or when the last one closes
  • Email when a contractor submits their prequalification
  • Insurance, accreditation and data sheet expiry dates on the registers
08
File and Document Hub

One place for all your H&S documents.

Stop emailing PDFs around or hunting through shared drives. The OSHE file hub lets you upload policies, procedures, toolbox talks, and any other documents into organised folders. Accessible from any device, instantly shared across your whole team.

  • Organised folder structure you control
  • Accessible from desktop and mobile
  • Any file type supported
  • Shared across your whole team instantly
09
Mobile App

Works offline. Built for site, not the office.

The OSHE mobile app keeps working without internet. Fill in forms, including incident notifications, complete task analyses with their hazards, and add tasks. Everything syncs when you get back in range. The desktop handles back-end management and reporting. The mobile app handles everything on site.

  • Forms, task analyses and tasks work offline
  • Auto-sync when back online
  • Incident notification form available offline
  • Hazards logged inside task analyses, even with no signal
  • Desktop handles back-end management and reporting
  • Install on any smartphone from the browser
